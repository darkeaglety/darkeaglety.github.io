1v1 stickman hoops on one device. First to 11 wins!

Controls
Coral (P1)
A D move
W jump
WW double-tap shoot
S steal / crossover
Teal (P2)
← → move
↑ jump
↑↑ double-tap shoot
↓ steal / crossover
Shots release at the top of your jump. Jump near the rim to DUNK. Hit 3 in a row to go ON FIRE. Esc pauses.
Coral plays on the left half of the screen.
Teal plays on the right half.
Slide on the arrow pad to run. JUMP to jump or block, SHOOT for a jump shot, STEAL to poke the ball (or crossover when you have it).
